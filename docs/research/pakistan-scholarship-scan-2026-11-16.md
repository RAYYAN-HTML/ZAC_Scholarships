The opportunity rows are staged in `scripts/import-research.ts`; the importer creates drafts with pending verification and never publishes. Do not treat this document as a substitute for the provider’s current instructions.

## Existing local/HEC cycle reviewed

- [HEC Balochistan and Erstwhile FATA Phase III, Batch V](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/scholarshipsgrants/BF-III/Pages/default.aspx) is open to eligible applicants, with a 14 Dec 2026 deadline; its current draft is already in the import file. HEC says admission/placement is the applicant’s responsibility and provides scholarship funding only.
- [HEC Chinese Government Scholarship Program 2027–28](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/scholarshipsgrants/lao/CGSP/Pages/How-to-Apply.aspx) is already in the import file. The HEC instructions require both HEC and CSC submissions and show an ETC test registration sub-deadline of 22 Nov 2026; the listing review note should preserve that nearer sub-deadline.
- HEC Need-Based Scholarships are administered through selected universities and their financial-aid offices; the HEC criteria page does not provide one nationwide current application window. Check each target institution before presenting it as open.
- [HEC Need-Based Scholarship Programme](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/scholarshipsgrants/NBS/Pages/Eligibility-Criteria.aspx) has no confirmed national deadline; applicants submit through participating university Financial Aid Offices. Its published criteria state tuition support and a PKR 6,000 monthly stipend. HEC pages differ on graduate eligibility, so the draft is unconfirmed until the target institution confirms its current intake and eligible level.
- Commonwealth Scholarships 2027–28 had a 20 Oct 2026 deadline and are closed as of this scan.
- Pakistan Economic Affairs Division’s [Japan Human Resource Development Scholarship (JDS) 2026 recruitment](https://www.ead.gov.pk/TrainingDetail/NWMzZDNlZjMtODQxYi00MWUwLWJjMzYtNDIzNzk1YTdkNzli) is open until 27 Nov 2026. This is an international postgraduate opportunity, but eligibility is restricted to qualifying Federal Government officers (cadre or ex-cadre BPS 17+ with at least three years of public-service work); it is not a general student scholarship. It is included in the research import with a strong eligibility warning.
- HEC’s LOA index still labels Kuwait University, Morocco and CGI 2026–27 opportunities active, but their dated notices do not show an open application now: Kuwait’s deadline was 12 Jun 2026, Morocco’s current notice is a nomination-status update, and CGI’s deadline was 31 Aug 2026. They were not staged as open opportunities. Check [Kuwait’s dated HEC announcement](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/HECAnnouncements/Pages/Kuwait-Scholarship.aspx), [Morocco nomination update](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/HECAnnouncements/Pages/NOMINATION-KMS.aspx), and [CGI 2027 announcement](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/HECAnnouncements/Pages/CGI.aspx).

## Verification notes

- Research date: 16 Nov 2026 UTC. Current-cycle status can change quickly; especially recheck deadlines near closing dates.
- Only official provider, government or host-university sources were used to stage the entries above. Third-party scholarship roundups were used only to identify leads, not to substantiate listing fields.
- Unknown award values, criteria and documents remain unspecified in the drafts. Exact fees, stipends or award levels are not inferred from prior years.
- The controlled import saves research records as unpublished drafts with pending verification. The local database was updated after the research sweep; production remains separate and must be migrated/imported deliberately.
- Providers reviewed in this first local pass include HEC and its Learning Opportunities Abroad portal, KPEF, PEEF, SEEF, BEEF, Punjab Honhaar/PHEC, SZABIST, ICAP, the University of Sindh, Isra University, LUMS NOP, Ramzan Talent Scholarship, Pakistan Bait-ul-Mal, Ministry of Religious Affairs, and the Scotland Pakistan Scholarship scheme.
