## Verification cautions

- These records have not been imported into MySQL or published. The import file writes them with `pending_verification` and `draft` states.
- HEC’s Chinese program page shows Jan 5, 2027 for HEC submission, while the How-to-Apply instructions also have a nearer ETC registration date (Nov 22, 2026). Preserve both dates in the student-facing listing after provider review.
- The BUP opportunity’s HEC announcement identifies an 80% tuition waiver and a maximum number of recipients, but contains inconsistent seat-count wording. Keep this flagged until BUP confirms the arrangement.
- For Aalto, KTH and Uppsala, tuition waivers do not cover living expenses. The scholarship display must not label them fully funded.
- Reach Oxford is not a currently available route for a new Oxford 2027 undergraduate admission applicant, because the university admission application closed. It is only relevant to candidates who obtain an offer and then meet the January scholarship deadline.
- The Erasmus Mundus student catalogue contains many separate programmes and each consortium sets its own application deadline. The three named Erasmus records above are individually confirmed programme-specific calls, not a complete Erasmus Mundus catalogue.
- JDS is included because it funds study in Japan and is recruited for Pakistan by EAD; its narrow public-service eligibility means it should be clearly distinguished from scholarships open to ordinary students.
- Research date: 16 Nov 2026 UTC. Recheck deadlines, application access and award details directly with the provider before marking any record verified.
